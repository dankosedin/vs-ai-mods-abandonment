## Gaps (everything that is not OK)

- #56 INLINE: "some authors added the same sentence to many mods". Checked ad hoc on 2026-11-11 from the private `data\private\extract-2026-11-02-all\ai-labels.csv` (USED labels from the description text, grouped by author and identical evidence quote): 16 author/quote groups cover 5 or more mods (largest 30, 17, 14, 12, 12, 12, ...); 59 groups cover 2 or more. This supports "the same sentence on many mods". That the sentence was ADDED later (after a release) is shown separately by the dating (#45: the largest late-declaring authors have 11, 8, 7 mods in the 1.22 cohort). No script or results file holds the grouping; a small script would turn it into OK.
- #63 INLINE: "it would take only about 10 wrong mods out of 45 to move 56% down to 44%". Arithmetic, no script: if x of the 45 AI-early mods were really late declarers that were updated (late ones update at 96%, so they are almost all updated), the early group becomes (25 - x) / (45 - x). x = 9 gives 16/36 = 44.4%; x = 10 gives 15/35 = 42.9%; so 9-10 mods. If the wrong mods were instead not updated, many more would be needed. The reverse direction (wrongly "late" mods really early) would raise 56%.
- Borderline rounding (not a mismatch): the post says other code mods are at 61% (#58). The stage 4b code/content report gives 892/1451 = 61.5% (61.47%), which rounds to 61%. In `author-bootstrap-...md` the same comparison group has 893/1452 = 61.5% (61.50%) because one code mod moved from noAI to rest after the noai-exclude decision. Under the new group definition the number would round to 62%; the difference is 0.03 points. `stage4b-code-content-...md` has not been regenerated for the exclusion (its own noAI definition is the old NOT_USED one).
- Wording, not numbers: the "current declarations" caveat for the AI share of new mods (#36) is stated only in the last objection ("by current declarations they are 8%, 12% and 24%"); the section-6 sentence with the same shares does not say it. Both use current (undated) labels, so late declarations push the recent quarters up.
- #48 uses current labels for the stated 4 in 10 against 3 in 10 (41% vs 29%, 22/54 vs 242/832, 2026H1, W=90); on dated labels only 19 of the 54 AI first mods can be dated (AI-early 5/13 = 38% vs 30% for everyone else), and the difference is not distinguishable from zero. The text word "roughly" and "not less often" fit both versions.
